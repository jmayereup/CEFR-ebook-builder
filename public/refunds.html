Refund Policy & Service Notice

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

CEFR Stories is currently offered as a free platform while building out our shared public story library. We do not charge subscription fees or process paid transactions on the platform.

1. Free Platform Access

Free story creation credits are provided for a limited time while our library is being built. Users who wish to generate additional stories beyond shared generation limits can bring their own API key from OpenRouter.

2. Payment Processing & Charges

Since we do not charge for access or collect subscription payments directly, there are no recurring subscription charges or refund processing fees required on our platform.

3. Contact Support

If you have any questions about platform usage or account management, please reach out to us at admin@teacherjake.com.
