Privacy Notice

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

CEFR Stories is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Notice describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in (e.g. using Google Sign-In), we receive your email address, name, and profile picture URL.
  • User-Generated Content: We store the parameters and text of the stories you generate, as well as review glossaries and vocabulary terms added to your practices collection. All generated stories are added to our public shared library catalog.
  • Reading History and Progress: We store your active reading placements (such as the last read chapter index) and the completion status of stories you read. If you are signed in, this data is saved to your account profile to sync your progress across devices; otherwise, it is stored locally on your browser's local storage.
  • Technical Diagnostics: To help us identify and troubleshoot issues, we log diagnostic metadata (such as model identifiers, action names, request duration, and technical error descriptions) exclusively when AI generation or translation requests fail. Any user email addresses linked to these logs are automatically and permanently masked (e.g. u***@domain.com) on our servers before storage.

2. Data Ownership and Deletion

We respect your ownership rights over your creative output:

  • Public Catalog: Stories created on CEFR Stories are public and published to the community catalog for language learners.
  • Story Deletion Requests: Users and community members can flag stories for deletion. Flagged stories are reviewed by administrators, who evaluate and execute permanent deletion from our databases in accordance with community guidelines.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver the language learning story generator service.
  • Support dictionary lookup operations and count API key transactions.
  • Enhance pedagogical grading configurations (using public stories or aggregates).

4. AI Integration (API Data Sharing and Prompt Retention)

When you generate a story outline, chapter, or vocabulary glossary, configuration prompts (such as target language, CEFR difficulty level, genre, and custom user notes) are sent to third-party AI inference providers (including OpenRouter, Meta, Google, Z-AI, and DeepSeek) to fulfill the generation request.

Identity and Anonymity: We do not attach your user name, email address, or user account ID to prompts or metadata transmitted to these third-party AI providers. For standard platform generations (including the free tier), requests are proxied through our servers and appear to AI providers as originating generically from our platform infrastructure.

Provider Prompt Retention & Model Training: Different AI models and provider endpoints have different data retention and model training policies:

  • Contributor & Training Tiers: Certain cost-efficient or free model endpoints (such as meta/muse-spark-1.3-contributor via OpenRouter) explicitly permit the model provider (e.g., Meta) to retain, log, and analyze prompt text and generated outputs to train and improve future AI systems. Although no personal user identity is attached to these requests, prompts sent to these models are subject to provider retention.
  • Standard & Frontier Models: Commercial standard models (such as meta/muse-spark-1.3 standard, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude) operate under strict zero-retention or non-training agreements where prompt inputs and outputs are not used for model training.

Users are advised never to submit sensitive personal data, proprietary secrets, or personally identifiable information within story prompt notes or custom instructions.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): If you configure a custom OpenRouter API key in Settings, generation requests are authenticated using your personal OpenRouter credentials. In this mode, usage and billing are associated directly with your OpenRouter account, and data handling is governed by OpenRouter's privacy policy and the specific model endpoints and data collection preferences you select on your OpenRouter account.

5. Google Advertising and Third-Party Cookies

We may display Google AdSense advertisements on our platform in the future to support free access. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can manage your advertising cookie choices directly using our Cookie Consent manager banner on our website.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your data privacy or request data extraction/removal, please feel free to reach out to us at admin@teacherjake.com.
